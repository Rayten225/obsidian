# Presert Continuous

## Theory
---

Sarah in her car. She is on her way to work. She's driving to work
This means: she's driving now, at the time of speaking. **The action is not finished**

| I am (= I'm)                 | driving    |
| ---------------------------- | ---------- |
| he/she/it is (=he's etc.)    | working    |
| we/you/they are (we're etc.) | doing etc. |
I'm soing something = **I started doing it and I haven't finished. In the middle of doing it.**

**But action may not happening at the time of speaking**
(I'm reading a really good book at the moment) - but not at the time of speaking

**You can use PC with today / this week / this year etc. (period around you)**

PC use when we talk about a change that has started to happen
- Is your English getting better?
- The population of the world is increasing very fast etc.
